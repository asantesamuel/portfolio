NovaYield

A farm-adaptation decision-support prototype exploring crop-rotation options with environmental context, local soil information, crop characteristics, and farmer priorities.

Contribution
Backend engineering & team leadership
Status
In development
Selected stack
FastAPI, Python, MongoDB, React / Vite

The context

Changing temperature and rainfall, water shortages, extreme weather, and declining soil health make farm planning more complex. NovaYield explores how NASA Earth observations can sit alongside local soil information, crop characteristics, and farmer priorities.

What the prototype explores

The concept is a decision-support tool for exploring crop-rotation options and their trade-offs. It is intended to support consideration and discussion around adaptation.

My contribution

I contribute backend engineering and team leadership to the project. The backend is partially implemented, and the frontend remains under development.

Current status

Work in progress. Backend partially implemented; frontend under development.

Project scope

NovaYield does not predict crop yields or provide definitive agronomic recommendations.
