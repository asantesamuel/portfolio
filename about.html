About Samuel

Curiosity, made practical.

I’m a builder at heart: I like taking a problem apart, understanding what needs to work, and creating useful software from what I learn.

I’m a Computer Science graduate from Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology, now building depth in software engineering with a particular focus on backend development.

I enjoy working on the parts of an application that help it behave reliably: its data, interfaces, and the way its pieces work together. Recent roles have given me experience with backend APIs, relational data, internal business software, and collaboration across engineering teams.

I’m also interested in AI and machine learning, especially where they can support useful, carefully considered applications. Backend engineering and strong software fundamentals remain the centre of my professional focus.

Outside project work, I value learning with others through leadership and professional-development communities.
